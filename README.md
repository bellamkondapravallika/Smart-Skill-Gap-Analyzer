🚀 Smart Skill Gap Analyzer

🎯 Identify Your Skill Gaps. Build Your Roadmap. Achieve Your Career Goals.

An AI-powered web application designed to help students and job seekers understand how well their skills align with a target job role. By analyzing resumes against job descriptions, the platform identifies missing skills and provides a personalized learning roadmap. 💡

📌 About the Project

The Smart Skill Gap Analyzer helps users evaluate their job readiness by comparing the skills extracted from their resumes with the skills required in a job description.

Instead of simply displaying a match percentage, it helps users understand what skills they already have, what they are missing, and what they can learn next. 🎓

✨ Key Features

- 🔐 User Authentication — Register and log in securely.
- 📄 Resume Upload — Upload your resume for skill analysis.
- 💼 Job Description Analysis — Enter the job description for your target role.
- 🧠 Skill Matching — Compare resume skills with job requirements.
- 📊 Match Score — View the estimated compatibility between your resume and the target job.
- 🔍 Missing Skills Identification — Discover skills that need improvement.
- 🗺️ Personalized Learning Roadmap — Follow a structured learning sequence based on identified skill gaps.

🔄 How It Works

1. 📝 Register / Login — Create an account or sign in.
2. 📤 Upload Resume — Upload your resume for analysis.
3. 💼 Enter Job Description — Provide the requirements for your desired job.
4. ⚙️ Analyze — Compare your resume skills with the job requirements.
5. 📈 View Match Score — Understand your current skill alignment.
6. 🎯 Identify Skill Gaps — Review missing or unmatched skills.
7. 📚 Get Learning Roadmap — Discover what to learn next.
